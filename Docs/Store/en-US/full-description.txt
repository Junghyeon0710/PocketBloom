A little pause. A garden in your pocket.

Pocket Bloom is a relaxing block puzzle. Place flower pieces on an 8×8 garden and complete rows or columns to make them bloom. Plan the order of your three pieces and clear several lines together.

JOURNEY
Meet score goals across 36 stages and collect stars.

ENDLESS GARDEN
Take your time and try to beat your personal best.

DAILY GARDEN
Enjoy a shared puzzle that changes with the UTC date.

MY GARDEN
Use the seeds you earn through play to grow your flower collection.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Find your next move with free hints, an undo, and a shuffle. Drag a piece into the garden, or tap a piece and then an empty cell.

Choose Korean or English, adjust music, sound and vibration, or turn on reduced motion. Core puzzle play works offline. There are no in-app purchases.

Your progress is stored on your current device. Uninstalling the app or changing devices may remove your progress. Cloud saves and online leaderboards are not included.
